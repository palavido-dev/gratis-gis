1mAndroid deltas

Same information architecture; platform conventions where they differ.

  • Navigation: same three destinations as a Material bottom navigation bar; predictive back replaces the sheet's swipe-down-to-dismiss, and hardware back closes the form with the same "discard draft?" confirm.
  • Controls: Material switches, radio buttons and the M3 segmented button in place of the iOS forms; the capture FAB becomes an M3 extended FAB on the Map tab so it carries the word "Add".
  • Camera: CameraX with the same GPS/bearing stamp. Many rugged Android handsets have a programmable hardware key — bind it to shutter and to My location.
  • Instruments: Bluetooth NMEA plus USB serial (OTG) receivers, and Android's mock-location provider so an external receiver can feed the whole OS, not just this app. iOS has no equivalent; on iOS the receiver feeds the app only.
  • Background work: WorkManager for queue drains and area downloads; foreground-service notification while a track log runs, which Android requires and iOS does not.
  • Storage: scoped storage means exports land in a SAF-picked folder rather than a Files-app container.

BRIEFGratisGIS Field — native app, round 1

What I read

  • docs/editing-and-collection.md — the data_collection item, map vs form mode, work areas, schema-versioned queues, conflict policy.
  • docs/data-collection-phase1.md — what shipped: 21 question types, IndexedDB outbox, clientId idempotency.
  • field/field-runtime.tsx (6.3k lines) — the PWA's real chrome: Layers FAB top-left, search, nav cluster + locate top-right, GPS accuracy strip, add-FAB bottom-right, crosshair reticle, template picker, form modal, More menu.
  • field/field-catalog.tsx, i18n/messages/en.ts — deployment rows, cache dots, Sync N chips, and the real offline/sync sentences. Copy in these screens is lifted from those strings, not invented.
  • docs/design-system.md + globals.css — Contour: warm paper neutrals, deep sage accent, Inter / Geist Mono, Lucide.

Assumptions I made

  • Contour, not the attached Industry system. This is your shipped product with a mandated palette; a native app that looks unlike the portal breaks principle 2. Say the word and I'll re-skin.
  • Primary user is the surveyor — long offline days, dozens of records, bright sun, one hand on the phone. So: no hover-dependent affordances, every primary control in the bottom-right thumb arc, 44pt minimum, and the GPS state permanently legible.
  • Two taps to anything. There is no settings tree. Device hardware (GPS source, offsets, laser, barcode) lives in one sheet reachable from the capture screen, because that is where you discover you need it.
  • Native earns its keep in four places: background location (track log survives screen-off), native camera pipeline, Bluetooth/serial peripherals, and local notifications when a queue drains or fails.
  • iOS shown. Android deltas are called out per screen where they differ.

The system

  • Three tabs, not a drawer: Collections · Map · Outbox. The outbox is a tab because unsynced work must never be something you have to go looking for; its badge is the only red in the app.
  • One status line. A single strip under every header carries connectivity + GPS accuracy + queue depth. It is the same strip everywhere, so it is never "buried in a menu".
  • Capture is map-first. Big sage FAB bottom-right, crosshair reticle tinted with the layer's own symbology, form rises as a sheet over the map so context is never lost.
  • Sun-legibility: ink on paper at 12.6:1, no grey-on-grey secondary text under 4.6:1, status conveyed by shape + label as well as hue.
